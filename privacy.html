Privacy

Prepared / Preparata: 4 September 2026

Who manages this website

The data controller is Matteo Icardi, an individual based in Italy. For privacy enquiries, contact matteo.icardi@outlook.com.

Browsing and technical data

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. Technical data, including IP address, requested page, browser and request time, are processed to deliver and secure the portfolio, based on legitimate interest. Vercel retains service data according to operational, security and legal needs; there is no single published retention period for all hosting logs. See its Privacy Notice. Processing may occur outside the EEA, including in the United States. Applicable transfer safeguards, including EU Standard Contractual Clauses, are described in Vercel’s Data Processing Addendum.

Language preference and cookies

The site stores your selected language in your browser under “portfolio-language”, using local storage. This preference is not an advertising identifier and is not sent to a marketing service by the website. It remains until overwritten or cleared through your browser’s site-data settings. The reviewed website code contains no advertising pixels or analytics scripts.

Fonts, images and videos

In this version, fonts, images and videos are served from the website itself, without embedding Google Fonts, YouTube or social-media players.

Contacting Matteo

This is a portfolio: it has no contact form, checkout or contract-management service. Email, WhatsApp and LinkedIn links open external services. If you contact Matteo, your name, contact details and message are used to respond to your enquiry, based on legitimate interest in professional correspondence or, where applicable, steps taken at your request before a contract. Providing information is voluntary, but sufficient contact details are needed to reply.

Email and messaging providers process communications through their services; their privacy notices apply when you use those platforms. Enquiries without follow-through are reviewed 24 months after the last contact and deleted if no longer needed. This is a manual review, not automatic deletion by the website. Any subsequent collaboration is handled outside this website.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact the email above. You may also complain to the data protection authority in the country of your habitual residence, workplace or alleged infringement. No automated decision-making or profiling functionality was identified in the reviewed website code.
