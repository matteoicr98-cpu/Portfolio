03 / Focus

Better events
start behind
the scenes.

This work focuses on the operational areas that make live events more prepared, inclusive and responsible.

Together, these focus areas shape how I approach events: creating clearer structures for teams, smoother experiences for visitors and stronger conditions for safe, well-run live operations.

Areas of focus

01 / Focus

Sustainability

Making sustainability part of everyday event operations, from crew awareness to practical actions on site.

01 / Focus

Sustainability

What it means

Turning sustainability goals into practical systems that work during live events.

In practice

  • Crew awareness & briefings
  • Visitor engagement
  • Waste & recycling systems
  • Resource-conscious operations
  • Sustainability training

My approach

Sustainability works best when it becomes part of the operation itself.

02 / Focus

Social Safety

Supporting safer dancefloors through visible awareness presence, visitor care and early intervention in situations involving harassment, discomfort or substance use.

02 / Focus

Social Safety

What it means

Creating a safer and more welcoming event environment through an awareness presence that stays visible, approachable and attentive to what is happening around visitors.

In practice

  • Mobile awareness presence
  • Identifying potentially unsafe situations
  • Harassment support
  • Substance-related wellbeing support
  • Supporting visitors who feel unsafe or overwhelmed
  • Appropriate intervention and escalation

Why it matters

Social safety creates the conditions for visitors to feel welcome, supported and able to enjoy the event with greater confidence and freedom.

03 / Focus

Visitor Experience

Looking at event operations from the visitor’s perspective — from arrival and access to support throughout the event.

03 / Focus

Visitor Experience

What it means

Understanding how operational decisions influence the way visitors experience an event.

In practice

  • Crowd and visitor flow
  • Wayfinding and visitor information
  • Queue and movement experience
  • Accessibility of help and support
  • Comfort and ease throughout the event
  • Identifying friction points in the visitor journey

Why it matters

Small operational decisions can have a major impact on how smooth, safe and welcoming an event feels.

04 / Focus

Crew Experience

Creating clear, supportive team environments where people understand their role, communication stays direct and crews can work effectively throughout the event.

04 / Focus

Crew Experience

What it means

Looking at crew operations not only as staffing, but as the experience people have while working within the event structure.

In practice

  • Clear roles and expectations
  • Team briefings
  • Direct communication
  • Accessible supervisor support
  • Shift clarity
  • Responding to team needs during live operations

Why it matters

Well-supported teams communicate better, respond faster and contribute to stronger event operations.
